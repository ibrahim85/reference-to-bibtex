Supported Formats

APA Style

Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title. Journal, Vol(Issue), pp-pp.

MLA Style

Author, First. "Title." Journal, vol. X, no. Y, Year, pp. XX-XX.

Vancouver

1. Author AA, Author BB. Title. Journal Year;Vol(Issue):pp-pp.

Tips

  • One reference per line works best
  • Ensure DOIs are included if available
  • Journal names should be spelled out or abbreviated consistently
  • Author names with commas help parsing accuracy
